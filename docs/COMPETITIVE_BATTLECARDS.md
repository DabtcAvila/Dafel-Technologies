## OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYBOOK

### "We're happy with our current solution"
**Response:** "That's great! Out of curiosity, what would need to change for you to consider alternatives? Many teams we work with were happy with Fivetran until their bills increased 70% with the new pricing model."

### "We don't have time for a migration"
**Response:** "I understand. That's why we handle the entire migration with zero downtime. Most customers complete the switch in 2-4 weeks while we guarantee better performance from day one. What if migration time wasn't a factor?"

### "Open source means less support"
**Response:** "Actually, it's the opposite. Because we're open-source, you get dedicated engineers who actually understand your deployment, not tier-1 support reading from scripts. Plus, you can see exactly how everything works."

### "We need enterprise features"
**Response:** "All our enterprise features are built-in: AES-256 encryption, SOC 2 ready architecture, RBAC, audit logging. What you don't get are enterprise vendor fees. What specific enterprise features are most important to you?"

### "The cost savings seem too good to be true"
**Response:** "I understand the skepticism. The difference is we don't have sales teams, marketing budgets, or VC returns to fund. You're paying for infrastructure and optionally support. Our technology is proven by 500+ production deployments processing petabytes monthly."
